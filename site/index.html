Revive & Thrive Team 🚀

Action Group Plan – Improving Sales & Employee Motivation

Problem Overview 📉

  • Sales have declined for 2 years. 📉
  • Staff layoffs due to poor performance. 😥
  • Low employee morale and engagement. 😞
  • Need: Practical strategies to improve sales & motivation. 💡

Goals 1 & 2 💪

Goal 1: Increase Sales (15% in 12 months) 📈

  • Short-term: Targeted promotions & discounts 🎁
  • Medium-term: Upselling & cross-selling training 📚
  • Long-term: Expand online presence/digital marketing 🌐

Goal 2: Boost Motivation & Retention ✨

  • Short-term: Recognition & reward program 🎉
  • Medium-term: Regular training & workshops 🧠
  • Long-term: Mentorship program 🤝

Goals 3 & 4 🎯

Goal 3: Enhance Customer Experience 😊

  • Short-term: Customer service refresher training 🗣️
  • Medium-term: Introduce customer feedback surveys 📝
  • Long-term: Loyalty program for repeat buyers 💖

Goal 4: Strengthen Collaboration 🤝

  • Short-term: Weekly cross-department meetings 🗓️
  • Medium-term: Adopt collaboration tools (Teams/Slack) 💬
  • Long-term: Annual team-building events 🥳

Goal 5: Encourage Innovation 💡

  • Short-term: Employee idea-sharing forum 🧠
  • Medium-term: Rewards for best ideas 🏆
  • Long-term: Innovation task groups to test new ideas 🔬

Summary & Expected Outcomes ✅

  • 15 Objectives across 5 goals.
  • Balanced short, medium & long-term strategies.
  • Increased sales performance.
  • Higher employee motivation & retention.
  • Stronger collaboration and innovation culture.
